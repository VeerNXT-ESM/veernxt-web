## How to review
1. Open a link in the table below (a sidebar lists the chapters; the dropdown at the top of the sidebar switches book). Any book can also be opened at a chapter with `&ch=N` on the end of the link.
2. For books with several subjects you will see a **cover page**, then a **contents page**, then the chapters. The cover picture comes from Admin > Subjects (replace it there).
3. The page is a basic reader: judge the **content, structure, lists, tables and images**, not colours or fonts.

## What to check in each book
- Chapters: is every chapter there, in the right order, with the right title? Is anything merged into another chapter, or split in two?
- Is the text identical to the DOCX (nothing missing, nothing added)? The check we ran says yes for every book below; please sanity-check a few chapters you know well.
- Bullets and numbering: do they match the DOCX, including indented sub-points?
- Images: right picture, right place.
- Tables: all columns and rows, no cut-off text. (Tables are now plain white with no outlines, as requested.)
- The cover page picture for each subject.
